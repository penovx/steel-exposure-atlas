READING RULE

Exposure is not a disruption probability. Country trade is not a buyer-supplier relationship. Missing ownership data is not proof that no parent exists.

ABOUT

A small study in public-data combination.

This project explores what can be made visible by connecting openly available industrial, trade, environmental and legal-entity data. It is a demonstrator, not a supplier-risk product, due-diligence service or operational decision system.

Every published layer is reviewed for reuse rights, cost, provenance and interpretation before it is shipped with the site.

Steel plant source: Global Energy Monitor, Global Iron and Steel Tracker, June 2026 (V1) release, distributed under CC BY 4.0. The atlas uses a transformed subset and identifies its own derived calculations separately. Global Energy Monitor does not endorse this project.
