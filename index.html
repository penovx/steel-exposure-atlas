READING RULE

Exposure is not a disruption probability. Country trade is not a buyer-supplier relationship. Matching parent text is a source-label match, not a resolved corporate hierarchy.

ABOUT

A small study in public-data combination.

This project explores what can be made visible by connecting openly available industrial, trade, environmental and legal-entity data. It is a demonstrator, not a supplier-risk product, due-diligence service or operational decision system.

Every published layer is reviewed for reuse rights, cost, provenance and interpretation before it is shipped with the site.
